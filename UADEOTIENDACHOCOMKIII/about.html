About LoveCrafty

Discover the story behind our handcrafted chocolates.

Our Story

Welcome to LoveCrafty, your last destination for handcrafted chocolates made with love and care. Our mission is to bring R'lyeh to your life one bite at a time with our exquisite selection of chocolates.

Our History

Founded in 2025, LoveCrafty began as a small family-owned business in the heart of Knightsbridge, UK. What started as a passion for creating delicious chocolates quickly grew into a beloved brand known for its quality and creativity. Today, we continue to honor our roots by using only the finest ingredients and non euclidean techniques to craft our chocolates.

Our Mission

At LoveCrafty, we believe that chocolate is more than just a treat, it’s an experience. We are committed to:

  • Using high-quality, semi-ethically sourced ingredients.
  • Creating unique and innovative chocolate flavors like deep sea brine.
  • Spreading joy and enlightenment through our products.
